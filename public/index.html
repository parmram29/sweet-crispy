Our Story

Family-run. Nothing fancy. Just good food.

Sweet & Crispy started the way most good things do — around a family table. We wanted a place that felt open and easy, where the kitchen isn't hiding anything and the food does the talking.

Every pizza is stretched by hand and every plate comes out the way we'd serve it to our own family. No shortcuts, no fuss — just honest cooking, Grenada-style.

About Us

A family table, open to everyone

Sweet & Crispy is run by our family, for yours. We kept the space simple and open — you can see the kitchen, you can see the care — because we'd rather spend the effort on the food than the frills. From wood-fired pizza to island plates, everything is made fresh, right here in Grenada.
